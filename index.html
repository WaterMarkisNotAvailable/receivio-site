The problem

Last-mile delivery breaks down when no one is home

Theft and weather

Parcels left unattended at lobbies or guard houses get lost, mixed up, rained on, or stolen.

Failed deliveries

With Cash-On-Delivery common in the Philippines, an absent recipient means another trip, more fuel, and more stress for riders.

Busy campuses

Students and faculty move between classes and offices all day, so meeting a courier on time is almost impossible.

How it works

From reservation to pickup in four steps

  1. 1

    Reserve a slot

    Pick a free vault in the Receivio app and give the address to your shop or courier.

  2. 2

    Courier drops it off

    The rider scans the parcel barcode at the kiosk, and your slot unlocks.

  3. 3

    The vault checks it

    A camera with a machine learning model and the slot sensors confirm a real parcel is inside before the door locks.

  4. 4

    You get notified

    A push notification with a photo arrives. Enter your retrieval passkey at the kiosk to open the slot.

Cash-On-Delivery orders are handled at the kiosk as walk-in deliveries.

The system

Four parts working together

Kiosk

  • PC with touchscreen
  • Camera with ML parcel check
  • Barcode scanner

Vault hardware

  • 10 parcel slots
  • ESP32 / Arduino controller
  • Ultrasonic sensors and reed switches
  • Solenoid locks and buzzer

Mobile app

  • Reserve a vault
  • Live vault status
  • Parcel history
  • Delivery alerts with photo

Cloud

  • Supabase database and accounts
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications

The study

About our research

Abstract

This study developed Receivio, a machine learning-enabled smart parcel receiving vault designed to address critical weaknesses in local last-mile delivery infrastructure. With the rapid expansion of the Philippine e-commerce market and a high reliance on Cash-On-Delivery (COD) payments, existing delivery models face significant challenges, including parcel theft, weather-induced damage, and increased operational costs for riders due to failed delivery attempts.

Receivio provides a secure, automated system to safeguard parcels, ensuring protection against environmental hazards and unauthorized access while streamlining delivery processes. The system leverages integrated sensing and machine learning to facilitate secure, automated package reception. This study assesses the effectiveness of the vault in lessening delivery risks and optimizing logistics workflows.

Preliminary results suggest that Receivio enhances parcel security and reduces failed delivery incidents, thereby improving overall logistical efficiency. Overall, Receivio serves as a functional prototype for enhancing last-mile delivery, with further developments focused on refining detection accuracy and system reliability.

Objectives

  1. Design and build an IoT-based parcel vault with automatic locking and separate compartments.
  2. Secure courier access in layers: barcode scanning, computer vision parcel detection, and Cash-On-Delivery handling.
  3. Send real-time delivery alerts and show vault status and occupancy in the Receivio mobile app.
  4. Evaluate the system: authentication speed, detection accuracy, user satisfaction, and trust.

Scope and limitations

Receivio is built for a university campus, serving students, teachers, and faculty staff. It covers the physical vault, the kiosk authentication, and the mobile app for monitoring and notifications.

  • Security starts once the parcel is inside the vault. What happens in transit stays with the courier.
  • Prepaid parcels come first. Cash-On-Delivery is supported as a secondary feature.
  • The vault checks that a parcel is present. It does not check what is inside the parcel.

Install

Get the app on your Android phone

The app is still being finished and tested. The download opens here once it is ready; these are the steps you will follow.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Tap Download APK on your phone. If your browser warns about the file type, choose Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Allow the install

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, turn on Allow from this source (Install unknown apps) for your browser, then go back.

  3. 3

    Install

    Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details and Install anyway. The app is not on the Play Store yet.

  4. 4

    Sign up and turn on alerts

    Open Receivio, create an account, then go to Settings → Notifications and enable notifications.
